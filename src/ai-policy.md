I don't like generative artificial intelligence (genAI).

I don't like that training them and using them requires immense use of natural resources and actively contributes to the climate crisis.

I don't like that they're overwhelmingly trained on other people's copyrighted content, without providing attribution or compensation.

I don't like that companies of all kinds are overwhelmingly trying to force them upon users who hate using them and have no means of disabling them.

I don't like that it's being used as an excuse to lay-off skilled people and avoid hiring and training junior staff, and as an uncritical replacement for real creativity and experience, despite being so often, plainly flawed.

**GenAI is bad for the environment, bad for creators, bad for customers, and bad for workers. GenAI has already killed people. GenAI has already destroyed communities. GenAI has already contributed to the rise of disinformation, fascism and authoritarianism.**

It's depressing that I need to write out all of the reasons why I don't want to use genAI, when the real question people should be asking is simply: is it worth the cost?

## Generative AI's use on this website

I avoid using genAI when coding, writing, or otherwise doing anything relating to this website.

I've previously dabbled in using (purportedly) responsibly trained image generation a little in the past, [like in this blog post]({{ '/blog/2024-01-01-2023-the-year-in-review/' | url }}), but have since stopped doing so. The licensing of training data doesn't negate the other negative effects, and the results were rubbish anyway.

Unfortunately, in [current year], avoiding 'AI' entirely isn't easy. My phone has an LLM built into it that I can't turn off and _might_ be used for things without being obvious or disclosed as such. So does my laptop. Has the object selection tool in Photoshop secretly been a genAI thing without them saying so? Has my operating system's spell checker silently been updated to start using an LLM without my knowledge?

I can't guarantee that everything will be certified organic and free of generative AI, I can only promise that I try to make it that way.
